The Work

Professional identity coaching — for moments that change everything.

This is for you if you're at a crossroads — building something new, navigating a career transition, finding your footing after an unexpected job loss, returning to work after a pause, or simply feeling the gap between who you are and how you're showing up.

01

What We Work On

We work intuitively and visionally — moving from emotional chaos to embodied clarity, and into a structure you can build on. This isn't therapy, and it isn't career coaching. It's the deeper work that makes both possible.

Topics include: professional identity, career transitions, job loss, re-entry after a pause, founder burnout, leadership presence, values alignment, and building toward what's next.

How It Works

Engagements are tailored to you — your context, your timeline, your pace. We typically work together over 3–6 months, with sessions every 2–3 weeks. Everything is confidential.

Every engagement begins with a discovery call to make sure it's the right fit. From there, we build something that's yours.
